## Overview

At **Zingplay Game Studio**, I worked on a **two-person team** delivering a major annual event for the Zingplay game portal in the Philippines and Vietnam. The event offered multiple minigames through which players could earn rewards.

## My contributions

- Implemented minigame logic and reward distribution.
- Optimized the service to accommodate approximately **10,000 concurrent users**.
- Developed internal testing controls to help frontend engineers and quality control teams exercise game scenarios.

## Technology

The service uses Java with Spring Boot and MySQL. My work focused on gameplay behavior, rewards, and service performance under concurrent load.
